Bumper Draws

Bumper Draws offer many of the biggest Indian lottery prizes, giving you the chance to dream of becoming a crorepati. They are special events which are usually held to celebrate important dates or festivals such as Holi, Diwali and the New Year.

You’ll find bumper draws wherever you are in India, from the Nagaland Dear Diwali Bumper to the Kerala Summer Bumper. They all work a bit differently but they usually give away loads of guaranteed awards, including top prizes worth multiple crore. Currently the Kerala Onam Bumper holds the record with a top prize of 25 Crore, but many other states are close behind. Take your pick from the states above to see all the available bumper lotteries.

Dear Lottery

Dear Lottery is a very common term used for lotteries in India and the name of the lottery often follows the word “Dear”. The majority of these draws take place in Nagaland and Punjab, where there are the Dear 1:00pm, Dear 6:00pm and Dear 8:00pm draws or the Dear Weekly and Dear Monthly lotteries, among many other dear lottery schemes. Head over to our Nagaland Lottery page or Punjab Lottery page to see our dedicated dear lottery results, prizes and more information on how all the lotteries function.
