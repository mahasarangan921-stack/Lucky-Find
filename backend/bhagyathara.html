How to Play Bhagyamithra Lottery

It’s simple to take part in the Bhagyamithra Lottery. Purchase a ticket featuring a random code. This code always starts with 'B' followed by another letter, then six numbers between 0 and 9.

The Bhagyamithra draw is conducted every Monday at 3:00 pm at Gorky Bhavan, near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram, under the authority of Kerala Lottery officials.

Store your ticket and sales receipt securely, as you will need both to claim your prize.

Players must be aged 18 or above to participate. You don’t need to live in Kerala to enter.

Prizes

The jackpot in the Bhagyamithra Lottery is worth ₹1 Crore, while there are also eight other prize categories. If you match the same six digits as the winning number for the first prize, but your ticket is from a different series (the second letter is different), you win a consolation prize of ₹5,000.

Claiming Prizes

Be sure to store both your lottery ticket and purchase receipt safely, as you’ll need them to claim any winnings. For added protection, fill in the required details on your ticket without delay.

If you win in the Bhagyamithra Lottery, you have 30 days from the draw date to claim your prize. Check your results often so you don’t miss your deadline.

The claiming process is straightforward but varies by prize amount. Winnings of up to ₹500 can be collected from a lottery agent. For prizes between ₹500 and ₹1,000, visit either a District Lottery Office or the Directorate of State Lotteries. First and second prize winners must go to the Directorate in person, as must winners living outside Kerala.

All India lottery prizes are taxed at 30 percent, as they are treated as taxable income.
